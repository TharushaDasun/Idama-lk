Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Idama.lk ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our property marketplace website.

Information we collect

  • Google account sign-in: when you log in as a seller using "Continue with Google", we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Listing information: details you provide when posting a property (title, price, location, description, contact number, photos).
  • Usage data: basic technical information needed to operate the site (such as session cookies that keep you logged in).

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your seller account
  • To display your property listings to buyers on Idama.lk
  • To let buyers contact you about a listing (via the contact details you choose to add to that listing)
  • To keep you signed in between visits

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
  • We do not post to your Google account or access your Gmail, Drive, or other Google services — only your basic name, email, and profile picture.

Data storage

Account and listing data is stored securely using Upstash (Redis) and hosted on Vercel. Photos you upload are stored with Cloudinary.

Your choices

You can log out at any time from your seller dashboard. To request that your account and listings be deleted, contact us using the details below.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at: contact@idama.lk
